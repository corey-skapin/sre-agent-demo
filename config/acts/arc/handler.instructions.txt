Arc application recovery policy
Only for an incident on {arcMachineName} whose Application events come from {eventSource} (4100, 4101 or 4200),
follow the arc-server-repair skill: diagnose with read-only queries, then propose exactly one Run Command that runs
the repair script installed on that server, as a Review card, and wait. Execute a change only after its specific
human approval in Review mode. Time the change from the run command's start time (the approved execution), never
from when the card appeared, and measure recovery from that time. Never name a clock time or a delay for a re-check,
and never say or imply that the alert will clear, or when. Do not apply this policy to SQL, to another machine or
to the Container App.
